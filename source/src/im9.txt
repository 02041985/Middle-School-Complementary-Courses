ts: Taming Fast Passages || Performance || How do I fix a difficult technical passage?
tx: Technical passage toolkit (written for this lesson): ¶ 1. Diagnose: play the passage once slowly. Circle the exact two or three notes where it breaks (often a hard finger change or register crossing). ¶ 2. Isolate the problem notes: play just the two notes back and forth 10 times smoothly. ¶ 3. Chunk: split the run into groups of 4 notes; play each group, pausing on the first note of the next group ("stop on the beat"). ¶ 4. Rhythm variations: play even sixteenths as long-short (dotted), then short-long. This trains each finger move. ¶ 5. Backward chaining: play the last group, then the last two groups, and so on to the beginning. ¶ 6. Tempo ladder: start at a tempo you can play 3 times perfectly; raise by 4 BPM; if you make a mistake, drop back 8. ¶ 7. Finger efficiency: watch in a mirror; keep fingers low and relaxed. ¶ Apply to one passage from the concert music; log start and end tempo.
