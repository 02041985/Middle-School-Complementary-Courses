tx: Precision stations (written for this lesson): ¶ Station 1, Measure and mark: Mark three cuts at 87 mm, 142 mm and 205 mm on a strip using a steel rule and a sharp pencil. Your partner checks each mark. Tolerance: ± 1 mm. ¶ Station 2, Kerf: Cut on the waste side of the line.
