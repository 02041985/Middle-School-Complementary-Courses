tx: Tone mark rules (written for this lesson): ¶ 1. If there is an a or e, it takes the mark: hǎo, xiè, gěi. ¶ 2. In ou, the o takes the mark: dōu, gǒu (dog). ¶ 3. Otherwise, the mark goes on the last vowel: in iu and ui, mark the second vowel: liù (six), duì (correct). ¶ 4. The dot on i disappears under a mark: nǐ, qī. ¶ Neutral tone: light and short, no mark: māma (mom), xièxie (thanks), bàba (dad). ¶ Third-tone change: when two third tones come together, the first becomes second tone: nǐ hǎo is said ní hǎo (but written nǐ hǎo). ¶ 不 bù change: before a fourth tone, bù becomes bú: bú shì (is not). ¶ Activities: 1.
