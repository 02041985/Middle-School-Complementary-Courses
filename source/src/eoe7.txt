Prevent: layers, stay dry, eat and drink, keep moving. Respond: get to shelter, replace wet clothes, add layers, warm sweet drinks if alert; severe cases (shivering stops, confusion): call 911. ¶ Frostnip and frostbite: skin turns white or greyish, feels numb or hard (cheeks, nose, ears, fingers).
