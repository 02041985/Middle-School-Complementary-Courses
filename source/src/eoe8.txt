Temperature (thermometer): cold water holds more oxygen; trout prefer cold water. 2. pH (test strips): many healthy streams range from about 6.5 to 8.5. 3. Survey participants and the partner: what worked, what to improve? ¶ 5. Impact report (one page): goals, what we did, numbers, photos, quotes, lessons learned, next steps. ¶ 6. Gratitude: send thank-you notes or a short video to the partner. ¶ 7. Share: post the impact report on the school website or newsletter with permission. ¶ 8. Follow-up monitoring: plan a return visit (e.g. in spring) to check seedling survival or weed regrowth at the same photo points.
