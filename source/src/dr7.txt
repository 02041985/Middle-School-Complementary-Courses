The audience retells the story afterward. Did they see the same objects you imagined?

tx: Warm-up and breath routine (written for this lesson): ¶ 1. Posture (2 minutes): Stand with feet hip-width apart, knees soft, shoulders relaxed, head balanced like a string pulls from the crown. ¶ 2. Find the diaphragm (3 minutes): Hands on your lower ribs and belly. Breathe in through the nose: your belly and ribs should expand, not your shoulders. Breathe out slowly on "sss." ¶ 3. Breath control (5 minutes): Breathe in for 4 counts, hold for 4, out on "sss" for 8. Increase to 12 and 16. ¶ 4. Body warm-up (3 minutes): Shoulder rolls, neck rolls (gently, side to side only), jaw massage, yawning stretch. ¶ 5. Voice warm-up (5 minutes): Lip trills (brrr) sliding up and down; humming on "mmm" and feeling the buzz in the face; sirens on "ng" from low to high. ¶ 6. Test (10 minutes): Say "Hello, my name is ___ and I'm here!" first with a shallow chest breath, then with a diaphragm breath. Compare the sound.

tx: Activities (written for this lesson): ¶ 1. Warm-up (5 minutes): Diaphragm breathing, lip trills and humming from last lesson. ¶ 2. Throw the ball (5 minutes): Hold an imaginary ball. Target practice (10 minutes): Stand at the front of the room. Say a line to the first row, the middle and then the back wall: "The train leaves at seven, and I'm not waiting for anyone." Discuss the difference in sound and in how the throat feels. ¶ 5. Whisper projection (10 minutes): Stage whispers must still be heard. Practise: "Shh, they're coming. Hide behind the curtain." ¶ 6. Dynamics (10 minutes): Perform a short scene where volume changes on purpose: a secret, an argument, a surprise.

hook: "Red leather, yellow leather." Say it five times fast. Actors drill tongue twisters because even a loud voice is useless if the words are mushy. Articulation turns sound into clear words.

Line delivery (15 minutes): Each student chooses a line from a list and delivers it with clear articulation, projection and a chosen pace. A partner at the back gives feedback on any word they missed.
