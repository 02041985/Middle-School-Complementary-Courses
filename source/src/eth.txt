v: ethical lens :: A way of looking at a moral question that focuses on one kind of consideration.

v: rights and duties lens :: Choosing the action that respects people's rights and keeps our duties and promises.
v: fairness lens :: Choosing the action that treats people equally or fairly.

Fairness: Which option treats people equally, or fairly given their situation? ¶ 4. Virtue: Which option shows the kind of person I want to be: honest, brave, kind, responsible? ¶ 5.
