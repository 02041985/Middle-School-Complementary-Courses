tx: Performance plan (written for this lesson): ¶ 1. Warm-up (5 minutes): Clap the pulse together; run each layer once. ¶ 2. Performance (15 minutes): Each group performs its piece for the class; the teacher records it on a tablet if possible. ¶ 3. Peer feedback (10 minutes): For each group, two listeners give one glow and one grow using criteria: steady tempo, accurate rhythms, clear entrances, dynamics, balance, ending together. ¶ 4. Listen back (10 minutes): Groups watch or listen to their recording. ¶ 5. Reflection (10 minutes): Each student rates the group 1–4 on each criterion and writes: one thing that went well, one thing to improve, and what you learned about composing. ¶ Audience etiquette: stay quiet during performances, applaud at the end and give specific, kind feedback.
