ts: Light and Shadow || Studio || How does value turn shapes into forms?
tx: Studio steps (written for this lesson): ¶ 1. Value scale (10 minutes): Draw a row of seven boxes. Leave the first white. Shade each box a little darker until the last is as dark as your pencil can go. Use a 2B or 4B pencil and build up layers rather than pressing hard at once. ¶ 2. Sphere study (15 minutes): Shine a lamp on a white ball from one side. Draw a circle. Find and shade: the highlight (lightest, nearest the light), the mid-tones, the core shadow (darkest band on the ball, away from the light), reflected light (a slightly lighter edge inside the shadow) and the cast shadow on the table. ¶ 3. Natural form (25 minutes): Light a natural form (shell, gourd, egg, pine cone) from one side. Draw its contour, then shade it with at least five values from your scale. ¶ Tip: squint at the object. tx: Project brief (written for this lesson): ¶ Create an original artwork, about 30 cm by 40 cm, that expresses a feeling or personal idea. It must include: at least one of your personal symbols; colours chosen for mood (warm, cool, complementary, tints or shades); expressive lines; a planned composition with a clear type of balance. ¶ Stage 1, Plan (one lesson): Choose your idea and feeling. Draw three thumbnails with notes on colours and balance. Get feedback from a partner using glow and grow. ¶ Stage 2, Create (two lessons): Choose your medium (paint, oil pastel, coloured pencil or mixed media). Work from your best thumbnail. ¶ Stage 3, Reflect (half a lesson): Write a short artist statement: what the work expresses, how colour, line and symbol show it, and what you learned.
